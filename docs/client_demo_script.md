# NexDrive client demo script

## 1. Customer experience (1 minute)

Open the public site, show live inventory filters and compare selection, then
start **Talk to Nex**. Explain that the same source of truth powers the website,
voice agent and operations team.

## 2. Discovery and recommendation (2 minutes)

Use: “I need a used AWD SUV under $30,000 with Apple CarPlay for a family of
five.” Show the returned vehicle IDs and why each recommendation fits. Change
the budget mid-call and verify that the agent searches again.

## 3. Safe transaction (2 minutes)

Select one vehicle, recheck availability, request Saturday slots, choose a
returned time and explicitly confirm. The agent must wait for the booking tool
before saying the test drive is booked.

## 4. Business evidence (2 minutes)

Open the protected portal. Show the lead, score, appointment, conversation
outcome and request trace. Point out that inventory status and pricing came from
Supabase rather than the language model.

## 5. Recovery and escalation (1 minute)

Ask for an unavailable slot or a sold vehicle. Show the alternative and then
request a human. Verify that the escalation record appears without fabricated
success.

## Closing statement

“Here is the conversation, the inventory it used, what the customer authorized,
the lead it created and the appointment now stored in the dealership system.”
